# Benchmark dataset manifest

The photos themselves are not committed (they may contain faces, plates or addresses). Record here what the
real dataset contains so the published results are reproducible by the owner.

| Folder | Count | Source |
|---|---|---|
| originals/ | — | taken by the builder on a phone (cars, walls, windows) |
| edits/ | — | generative inpaint edits: add a dent, remove a crack, add a water stain |
| negatives/ | — | near-duplicate different scenes (same car model, same room type) |
| whatsapp/ | — | originals after a real WhatsApp send/receive |
| x/ | — | originals after a real X upload/download |
